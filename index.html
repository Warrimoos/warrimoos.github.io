Our story

From Port Macquarie, with love.

Warrimoo is a tiny app studio in Port Macquarie, on the beaches of the New South Wales Mid North Coast. It's run by a veterinarian who kept watching loving owners juggle fridge magnets, sticky notes and guesswork: did Bella get her tablet this morning?

So we're building the apps the consult room keeps asking for. Small, focused, honest tools that respect your time, your privacy, and the animals snoozing at your feet while you read this.
